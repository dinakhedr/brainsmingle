What is Signal

One domain.
One week.
One room.

Signal by BrainsMingle is a domain-focused global virtual event, bringing together practitioners, founders, researchers, and leaders across the globe for live sessions, panels, special activations, and a full-day speed networking for thousands of connections.

That's not networking.
That's momentum.
Live Sessions & Panels
More than 100 sessions across five dedicated tracks featuring practitioners, researchers, founders, and industry leaders. Learn how AI is transforming industries and discover practical strategies you can apply immediately.
Special Activations
Signal Launchpad for startups. Signal Talent for hiring. Dedicated experiences built to create opportunities, partnerships, and career growth across the AI ecosystem.
Speed Networking Day
A full day dedicated to connecting attendees from across the event. Meet founders, developers, recruiters, researchers, and professionals in structured networking sessions designed to spark meaningful conversations.
Fully Virtual. Truly Global.
Attend from anywhere in the world. No travel costs, no logistics, and no barriers to joining one of the fastest-growing AI communities.
