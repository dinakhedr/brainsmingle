4. Sponsors and partners

This section matters, so it is deliberately blunt.

4.1 What sponsors always receive

Aggregate, anonymous statistics. For example: how many people attended a session, the mix of countries, the mix of attendee types and career levels. These figures never identify you.

4.2 What sponsors receive only with your consent

If you tick the optional consent box at registration, we share your name, email, job title, career level, company, country, attendee type and primary track with our sponsors and partners so they can contact you about their products and services.

Ticking that box is entirely optional. You get full access to the event either way. Once shared, each sponsor becomes an independent controller of that data and their own privacy policy applies to what they do with it.

4.3 Withdrawing

You can withdraw consent at any time in your account settings, using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or by writing to [PRIVACY EMAIL]. Withdrawal stops future sharing. To have data already shared deleted, contact the sponsor directly, and we will tell you who received it if you ask.

4.4 Interacting with a sponsor during the event

If you visit a sponsor's virtual space, request a demo, or join a sponsored session, that sponsor is told you were there. That is separate from the consent box above and follows from your own action.

5. What other attendees can see

The event is a networking event, so parts of your profile are visible to other participants: your name, job title, company, country, attendee type, primary track, and your photo and biography if you add them.

Your email address is never shown to other attendees. It is shared only if you choose to connect with someone and share it.

In speed networking sessions, the person you are matched with sees your profile and your networking goals so the conversation has a starting point.

6. Recordings

Sessions, panels and activations are recorded. If you turn on your camera or microphone, ask a question or post in a session chat, you may appear in the recording.

Recordings may be published on demand after the event, used in promotional material, and shared with sponsors and partners.

To avoid appearing, keep your camera and microphone off and use the anonymous question option where one is available. One-to-one networking conversations are not recorded by us.

If you appear in a recording and want it removed, write to [PRIVACY EMAIL] and we will assess what is technically possible.
