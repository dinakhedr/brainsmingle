Manage payments

Last updated: July 2026

View all payments made on your platform and manage payout requests from hosts and experts. Search and filter transactions by event, organizer, participant, or payment type.

Open the Payments page

  1. Open the Admin Panel.
  2. Click Payments in the left sidebar.

The Platform Payments page displays two sections: All Payments and Payout Requests.

View and search payments

The All Payments section lists every payment transaction on the platform. Four summary cards at the top display your Total Revenue, Platform Fees, Pending Payouts, and Organizer Earnings.

Each payment record includes:

  • Type — whether the transaction is from an Event or a Consultation.
  • Event/Consultation — the name of the session.
  • Organizer — the host who created the session.
  • Participant — the attendee who made the payment.
  • Amount — the total amount paid.
  • Platform Fee — the fee retained by the platform.
  • Organizer Earnings — the amount the organizer receives after the platform fee.
  • Date — when the payment was made.

Search and filter payments

  1. Type an event name, organizer name, or participant name in the search field and click Search.
  2. Use the All Types dropdown to filter by payment type: Events Only or Consultations Only.

Manage payout requests

The Payout Requests section shows withdrawal requests submitted by hosts and experts who have earned revenue on the platform.

Each payout request includes the organizer name, amount, current status, the date requested, and the date processed (if applicable).

Filter by status

Use the All Status dropdown to filter payout requests by their current status:

  • Pending — the request is awaiting admin review.
  • Approved — the request has been approved and is being processed.
  • Completed — the payout has been successfully transferred.
  • Rejected — the request was declined by an admin.

Approve or reject a request

  1. Find the payout request you want to review.
  2. Click the checkmark button to approve, or the X button to reject.
Tip: Hosts and experts must have a connected payment provider (e.g. Stripe) before they can request payouts. See Configure payment providers for setup details.
