Send bulk emails

Last updated: July 2026

Send personalized emails to multiple recipients at once. Write your subject and content using HTML, add optional tracking tags, and choose to email a custom CSV list or all active users on the platform.

Open the Bulk Email page

  1. Open the Admin Panel.
  2. Click Bulk Email in the left sidebar.

The Bulk Email page displays all the fields needed to compose and send a personalized email to multiple recipients.

At the top, a How it works box summarizes the process: upload a CSV file with firstName and email columns, use the {{firstName}} placeholder for personalization, and emails are sent individually with a small delay to avoid rate limiting.

Write the email subject

  1. Under Email Subject, enter the subject line for your email.

Write the email content

  1. Under Email Content, enter the body of your email using HTML markup.
  2. Use the {{firstName}} placeholder anywhere in your content to insert each recipient's first name automatically.

The content field supports up to 10,000 characters. A character counter is displayed below the field.

Tip: Write your email content in HTML. For example, use <div>Hello {{firstName}},</div> to start with a personalized greeting. The platform renders the HTML in the recipient's inbox.

Add tags (optional)

  1. Under Tags (Optional), type a tag name in the input field and press Enter or click Add.
  2. Repeat to add more tags. You can add up to 5 tags per email.

Tags are used for Mailgun tracking and analytics. They help you filter and measure email performance in your Mailgun dashboard.

Choose recipients

Under Recipients, select one of two options:

    • Upload CSV — email a custom list of recipients. Select this option and upload a CSV file containing firstName and email columns.
    • All active users — email everyone on the platform. The card shows the total number of recipients (e.g. 44 recipients).

When Upload CSV is selected, a CSV Upload area appears below. Drag your CSV file into the upload area or click to browse. Click Download example CSV in the top-right corner of the upload area to get a sample file with the correct column format.

Send the email

  1. Review your subject, content, and recipient selection.
  2. Click Send Bulk Emails at the bottom of the page.

Emails are sent individually to each recipient with a small delay between sends to avoid rate limiting. Each recipient receives a separate email with their personalized content.

Please note: Bulk emails cannot be recalled once sent. Double-check your subject, content, and recipient list before clicking Send Bulk Emails.
