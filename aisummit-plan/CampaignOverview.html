The seven phases — at a glance

Phase 0

Foundation

Sun 23 Aug – 5 Sep

The private, supply-side phase. Before any public noise, we reach a launch-ready threshold — enough speakers, partners and sponsors to impress — and build every campaign asset so launch day fires cleanly and the pages can update daily afterward. Recruitment doesn't end at launch; it runs continuously through the campaign.

The partner volume-discount is a private commercial term — it lives in outreach, never in public copy.

Objectives

  • Reach a launch-ready speaker threshold (~70) with assets, on a daily-updatable page
  • Sign partners onto the referral model and arm them with links, kits and outreach templates
  • Lock confirmed sponsors on the combined partners & sponsors page
  • Build the full campaign asset library — launch, teaser and templated Build sets
Phase 1

Teaser

6 – 9 Sep

A four-day curiosity burst. No name, no date, no registration link — just the sense that the Arab AI world is about to get a room of its own. The window is tight, so this is 3–4 high-impact posts, not a slow build.

Objectives

  • Create curiosity and anticipation ahead of the reveal
  • Grow the follower and notification base before registration opens
  • Plant the core idea — real conversation, a real room — without naming the event
  • Prime the audience to act the moment launch hits
Phase 2

Launch

10 Sep

The reveal, in one coordinated drop. Name, dates, five tracks, nightly Speed Networking, and free registration all go live the same day. Founders, speakers and partners post in parallel, and partners drop their referral links.

Objectives

  • Reveal the summit in full and open free registration
  • Drive the first and largest wave of signups
  • Activate every partner to post their referral link simultaneously
  • Set the positioning in one line: free, 7 days, the room where everyone finally meets
Phase 3

Build

11 Sep – 6 Oct

The engine room — nearly four weeks that carry the whole campaign, on a repeating weekly posting template plus scheduled email batches. Speaker announcements, the five track spotlights, why-attend angles, daily page updates and the nightly Speed Networking hook. The job is turning a free, low-commitment signup into a real intention to show up.

Objectives

  • Drive the bulk of registrations across the month
  • Establish speaker and session credibility through daily roster reveals
  • Build awareness of all five tracks and who each is for
  • Make Speed Networking the reason to attend, not just to register
  • Keep partners active and referral-driven signups flowing
  • Reveal the full agenda ~10 days out as a marquee moment
Phase 4

Final push

7 – 9 Oct

A 72-hour urgency sprint. Everyone who has been watching but not registered gets pulled in, while the already-registered get reminded why night one matters. Opening Day lineup revealed, countdown energy, and the low-friction close: it's free — be in the room.

Objectives

  • Capture last-minute registrations before the summit goes live
  • Reveal the Opening Day lineup to spike desire
  • Re-activate registered attendees so they actually turn up on Day 1
  • Trigger partners' final-call posts
Phase 5

During the event

10 – 16 Oct

Live for seven days. The daily rhythm: today's tracks, then the 9 PM Day Spotlight, then the 10 PM Speed Networking wrap — building all week to Friday's full-day finale. Two jobs at once: retain the people who are in, and create FOMO for those who aren't. Free registration stays open, so mid-week signups still count.

Objectives

  • Retain and re-activate registered attendees every single day
  • Keep driving free registration through the week via live FOMO
  • Showcase Speed Networking nightly as the signature moment
  • Capture live proof for reuse in Post Event
  • Build momentum toward Speed Networking Day as the peak
Phase 6

Post event

17 – 20 Oct

Capture the proof and turn a week-long event into lasting connection. Recap the numbers, thank the community, and carry the momentum home to BrainsMingle — for attendees and partners both — while planting the seed for the next edition.

Objectives

  • Publish proof and impact: registrants, countries, sessions, connections
  • Convert attendees onto BrainsMingle, the home the summit lived in
  • Deepen partner relationships and transition them toward the platform
  • Seed anticipation for Edition 02

Phase 4 · Final push — in detail

Planned

72 hours, one job: turn watchers into registrations and registrations into attendance. Nothing new gets built here — it's urgency, the Opening Day reveal, and the lowest-friction close in the campaign.

Two audiences, 72 hours

The not-yet-registered get pulled in; the already-registered get reminded why night one matters. Every asset targets one or the other.

Opening Day is the only new reveal

The Day 1 lineup is the single spike of the phase. It sells desire, not information — everything else is already public.

Free is the close

There's no price objection to overcome — the only barrier is attention. “It's free — be in the room” is the whole ask.

Registration never closes

Signups stay open right through the summit week — so the push is about urgency, not a deadline that doesn't exist.
