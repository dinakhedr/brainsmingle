Service Creation

Define what you offer. Manage it all in one dashboard.

The Consultation Services dashboard is where experts create, edit, and manage every service they offer. Each service card shows its title, description, duration, price, and visibility — Public or Private Link. Share your services to X, LinkedIn, or copy the link directly. Availability settings sit right below, showing which days and time slots are open for booking.

  • Service cards — each service displays its description, duration, price, and Public or Private Link visibility at a glance.
  • Free or paid — run a free intro call alongside a paid deep-dive without creating separate pages.
  • Quick actions — duplicate, edit, or manage any service directly from its card.
  • Availability below — day-by-day time slots displayed on the same page so services and schedule live together.
Consultation Services — service cards, share buttons, and availability in one dashboard
Availability

Set your hours once. Clients only see open slots.

The Availability Settings section sits directly below your services. Pick the days you're available, add one or more time ranges per day, and save. Clients see only the slots that are open when they book — no back-and-forth emails, no calendar sync required. Edit anytime with a single click.

  • Day-by-day control — toggle each day on or off and set independent time ranges for each.
  • Multiple time ranges — split a day into morning and afternoon blocks to protect breaks.
  • Timezone-aware — experts set availability in their timezone; clients see times converted to theirs.
Availability Settings — day-by-day time ranges with one-click editing
Visibility Control

Control exactly who finds each service.

Every service has its own visibility setting with three options — Public, Profile, or Private. Make a service findable to anyone, limit it to your profile page, or lock it to a direct link. Because it's set per service, a public strategy session can run alongside a private intro call reserved for specific clients.

  • Public — anyone can find and book the service through search and discovery.
  • Profile — the service shows only on your profile page, not in wider search.
  • Private — hidden everywhere except a direct link you share with specific clients.
Service visibility — Public, Profile, or Private, set per service
Pricing

Price in USD, EGP, or offer it free.

Each service card displays its price and duration right on the dashboard. Set a fee in USD, EGP, or both — or toggle the service to free for discovery calls and intro sessions. Clients pay at booking. No invoices, no chasing, no currency conversion plugins.

  • Dual currency — set pricing in USD, EGP, or both on the same service.
  • Free toggle — offer complimentary sessions alongside paid ones from the same dashboard.
  • Automatic collection — payments are processed at booking. No manual invoicing or reconciliation.
Mixed pricing — paid and free services with duration on every card
Client Requests

Review, respond, and manage every incoming request.

When a client books a consultation, the request lands in the Client Requests tab with everything you need to decide: the service they chose, their message, preferred date and time, duration, and price. Accept with a response and the session page is created automatically. Decline with a note and the client is notified instantly.

  • Full request context — service title, price, duration, client message, and proposed schedule in one card.
  • Accept or Decline — respond to each request with a personal message the client can read.
  • Auto-generated session — accepted requests create a session page with a direct link and Add to Calendar.
  • Three statuses — Pending, Accepted, and Rejected visible at a glance across all requests.
Client Requests — review incoming bookings, respond, and track status
My Bookings

Track every consultation you've requested.

The My Bookings tab shows every consultation request you've sent to other experts. Each card shows the expert's name, your original message, the proposed schedule, and real-time status. Pending requests can be cancelled. Accepted bookings link directly to the session page and calendar. Declined requests show the expert's response so you know why.

  • Two-way flow — experts manage requests in Client Requests; clients track them in My Bookings.
  • Cancel anytime — withdraw a pending request before the expert responds.
  • Session access — accepted bookings surface Go to session page and Add to Calendar in one click.
  • Expert responses visible — see the expert's acceptance note or reason for declining directly on the card.
My Bookings — track outgoing requests, cancel pending ones, and join accepted sessions
Earnings & Payouts

Earn from expertise. Get paid directly.

Every paid consultation and paid session flows into the My Earnings dashboard. Four cards show Total Earnings, Available for Payout, Pending, and Completed at a glance. Connect Stripe once, then request payouts whenever your available balance is ready. The Recent Activity log tracks every transaction — consultations, paid rooms, and payouts — with date, amount, and status.

  • Two earning sources — paid consultations and paid video rooms both feed into one dashboard.
  • One-click payout — connect Stripe once, then request payouts directly to your bank account.
  • Activity log — filter by All, Earnings, or Payouts to see every transaction with date, amount, and status.
  • Revenue share — experts retain 85–90% of every fee. No invoicing. No reconciliation.
My Earnings — metrics, Stripe payouts, and full activity log in one view
