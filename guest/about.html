About BrainsMingle

Building the professional platform everyone deserves.

BrainsMingle is where professionals meet face to face, share what they know, and earn from their expertise, no matter where in the world they are.

The story

Two engineers, two continents, one frustration.

They kept hitting the same wall from opposite sides of the world, so they built the platform neither of them could find.

Belal was in Oulu. Yousef was in Toronto. Different corners of the world, different problems in front of them, and the same complaint about how professionals are supposed to find each other.

Connection without conversation

Professional networks had become places to accumulate contacts rather than talk to them. You could have hundreds of connections and never once have a real conversation with any of them. Meanwhile the people with genuinely useful knowledge, the ones worth an hour of your time, were unreachable behind the same feeds and inboxes as everyone else.

Six tools, none of them talking

The second problem was quieter but just as costly. Professionals who did want to share what they knew had to assemble a business out of six unrelated tools: one for video, another for scheduling, another for payments, another for hosting a course, another for the community, another for the forms. None of them spoke to each other, and none of them were built for the person doing all six jobs at once.

What they built

So they built one platform for both halves. Video-first, so every connection starts as an actual conversation. And complete, so the expertise you share can also be a living.

What we believe

What the frustration taught us.

Four commitments that decide what goes into BrainsMingle, and what stays out.

Every connection should be a conversation.

A contact you've never spoken to isn't a connection. We put video first so that meeting someone means actually meeting them, not exchanging profiles and hoping.

Expertise deserves a living.

Knowing something valuable shouldn't require a separate business to make money from it. Consultations, sessions, courses, and payments belong in the same place as the audience.

One place, not six.

Every tool you bolt on is another login, another integration, another thing that breaks. We'd rather build the whole thing properly than be one more tab.

Built for everyone, everywhere.

A professional in Cairo should get the same platform as one in Toronto, same features, same quality, right language, right direction on the page. Not a translation layer added later.

The road ahead

We're just getting started.

BrainsMingle is live, growing, and nowhere near finished. New professionals arrive every week to teach, consult, and build communities around what they know, and each one moves us closer to a world where good knowledge always finds the people who need it. That's the whole reason we build. Come help us get there faster.
