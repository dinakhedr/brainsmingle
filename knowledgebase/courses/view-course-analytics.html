View course analytics

Last updated: July 2026

Track enrollment, completion rates, and learner progress for your courses. Access analytics from the Courses tab or from inside a specific course.

Before you get started

  • You must be a community admin or Suite Admin.
  • Your community must have the Courses tab visible. See Set up your community to enable it.

Access analytics from the Courses tab

  1. Open your community and click Courses in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Analytics in the top toolbar to view analytics across all courses and programs.

Access analytics for a specific course

  1. Open a course by clicking the course card.
  2. Click Analytics in the top toolbar to view analytics for that specific course.
Tip: Use course-level analytics to identify lessons with low completion rates and improve your content accordingly.
