Set up certification

Last updated: July 2026

Award certificates to members who complete courses or accumulate a minimum number of learning hours. Upload a custom certificate background, set completion thresholds, and let the platform issue certificates automatically.

Before you get started

  • You must be a community admin or Suite Admin.
  • Your community must have the Courses tab visible. See Set up your community to enable it.

Open certification settings

  1. Open your community and click Courses in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Certificate in the top toolbar.

The Certification settings dialog has two controls: Use a custom certificate design and Enable certification.

Upload a custom certificate design

  1. Toggle Use a custom certificate design on.
  2. Drag your certificate background image into the upload area, or click to browse. The image must be PNG or JPG, landscape A4, approximately 1600×1131 px.
  3. Select the text color:
    • Dark text — for light certificate backgrounds.
    • Light text — for dark certificate backgrounds.
  4. Check the Preview panel on the right to see where the certificate text appears. The platform automatically places the recipient name, course title, completion date, certificate ID, and verify link.

Enable certification and set thresholds

  1. Toggle Enable certification on.
  2. Enter a Certificate title.
  3. Set the completion threshold using one or both options:
    • Minimum courses completed — members must complete at least this many courses.
    • Minimum hours of coursework — members must accumulate at least this many hours of learning.
  4. Click Save.

Members who meet either threshold receive a certificate automatically.

Tip: You can set both thresholds — members who meet either one qualify for the certificate. Use Minimum courses completed for structured programs and Minimum hours of coursework for self-paced learning paths.
