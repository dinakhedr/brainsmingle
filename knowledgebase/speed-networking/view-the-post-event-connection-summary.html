View the post-event connection summary

Last updated: July 2026

After a speed networking session ends, every participant receives a connection summary showing the people they met. Use it to follow up, send messages, and grow your professional network.

How it works

  1. When the speed networking session ends, the platform automatically generates a connection summary for each participant.
  2. The summary lists every person you were matched with during the session, including their name, headline, and profile link.
  3. Click a person's name or profile link to view their full profile and send them a direct message.
Tip: Follow up within 24 hours while the conversation is still fresh. Use the direct message feature to continue discussions started during the speed networking session.

Access the summary as an admin

  1. Navigate to the room detail page by clicking the room title from your dashboard.
  2. Click the Discussions tab to view post-event discussions and connection activity.
  3. Click the Members tab to see the full list of participants who attended the session.
Tip: As an admin, use the participant list to measure engagement and plan future networking events based on attendance patterns.
