Manage your profile and earnings

Last updated: July 2026

Your profile is your public-facing page on BrainsMingle — it shows your bio, consultation services, and availability in one place. From here you can also track your earnings, connect Stripe to receive payouts, and create paid rooms to start earning.

Before you get started

  • You need an active BrainsMingle account.
  • To receive payouts for paid services and paid rooms, you need a Stripe account. Stripe setup is a one‑time process.

Review your profile

Your profile page displays your photo, name, headline, bio, social links, and follower count at the top. Below that, the tab bar gives you access to your Professional Network, Rooms You Manage, Rooms You Joined, Feedback, and Services.

  1. Click your avatar or name in the navigation to open your profile.
  2. To edit your bio, click the edit icon () next to About.
  3. To view or copy your public profile link, click View public profile or Copy profile link in the top action bar.
  4. To invite people to your network, click Invite Network.
Tip: Click the Services tab to see your consultation services and availability directly from your profile. This is the same view your clients see when they visit your public profile.

Track your earnings

  1. Click My Earnings in the top action bar on your profile page.
  2. The earnings dashboard shows four metrics: Total Earnings, Available for Payout, Pending Payout, and Completed Payouts.

From the earnings page you can take two quick actions:

  1. Click Create Paid Room to create a new room and start earning. This opens the Create New Room page where you select an event type.
  2. Click Offer Consultation to go to your consultation services setup.

Connect Stripe to receive payouts

  1. On the My Earnings page, click Connect Stripe to Receive Payouts.
  2. In the Set Up Payouts modal, select your Country (the country where your bank account is located).
  3. Click Connect Stripe. To skip this step for now, click Maybe Later.
  1. You are redirected to Stripe's onboarding page. Enter your Email address and Phone number, then click Submit.
  2. Complete the remaining Stripe verification steps. Once verified, payouts are deposited directly to your bank account.
Please note: Stripe setup is a one‑time process. You must complete Stripe verification before you can receive payouts for paid consultations and paid rooms.
