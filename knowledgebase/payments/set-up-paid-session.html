Set up paid session

Last updated: August 2026

Charge attendees to join your live video sessions. Set a ticket price in USD or EGP, review the platform fee breakdown, and optionally attach discount codes to offer promotions.

Before you get started

  • You must have a connected payment account to receive payouts. See Connect Stripe and receive payouts.
  • You need an existing video room or be in the process of creating one. See Schedule a live session.

Enable paid access

  1. While creating or editing a video room, scroll to the Access & Pricing section.
  2. Find the Paid Access toggle.
  3. Toggle from Free to Paid.
  4. Enter the price in USD, EGP, or both. Attendees pay in the currency that fits them.

BrainsMingle charges a 10% platform fee on paid sessions. The net amount you receive per attendee is displayed below the price field.

Attach discount codes (optional)

The Discount codes section appears below the price field when paid access is enabled. It shows any codes that automatically apply to this session.

If no codes exist yet, you can click Create a discount code to set one up on the Coupons page. When a code is active, it appears with its discount percentage and an Always applies badge.

Tip: To create or manage discount codes, see Create and manage coupons.
