Track your earnings

Last updated: July 2026

View your total earnings, available balance, pending payouts, and completed payouts from one dashboard. You can also create a paid room or offer a consultation directly from the earnings page.

Before you get started

  • You need an active BrainsMingle account.
  • To receive payouts, you must connect your Stripe account. See Connect Stripe and receive payouts.

Open the earnings dashboard

  1. Click your avatar or name in the navigation to open your profile.
  2. Click My Earnings in the top action bar.
  3. The dashboard shows four metrics:
    • Total Earnings — your lifetime earnings on the platform.
    • Available for Payout — the amount ready to be transferred to your bank account.
    • Pending Payout — payouts currently being processed.
    • Completed Payouts — total amount already deposited to your bank account.

Take quick actions from the earnings page

  1. Click Create Paid Room to create a new room and start earning. This opens the Create New Room page where you select an event type.
  2. Click Offer Consultation to go to your consultation services setup.
Tip: Check your earnings dashboard regularly to monitor your available balance and payout status.
