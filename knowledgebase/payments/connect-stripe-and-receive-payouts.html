Connect Stripe and receive payouts

Last updated: July 2026

Connect your Stripe account to receive payouts for paid consultations, paid rooms, and paid community memberships. Stripe setup is a one‑time process — once verified, payouts are deposited directly to your bank account.

Before you get started

  • You need an active BrainsMingle account.
  • You need a bank account in a Stripe‑supported country.

Open the My Earnings page

  1. Click your avatar or name in the navigation to open your profile.
  2. Click My Earnings in the top action bar.

Connect your Stripe account

  1. On the My Earnings page, click Connect Stripe to Receive Payouts.
  2. In the Set Up Payouts modal, select your Country (the country where your bank account is located).
  3. Click Connect Stripe. To skip this step for now, click Maybe Later.
  1. You are redirected to Stripe's onboarding page. Enter your Email address and Phone number, then click Submit.
  2. Complete the remaining Stripe verification steps. Once verified, payouts are deposited directly to your bank account.
Please note: Stripe setup is a one‑time process. You must complete Stripe verification before you can receive payouts for paid consultations, paid rooms, and paid community memberships.

After Stripe is connected

Once your Stripe account is verified, the My Earnings dashboard displays your earnings metrics and quick actions.

Tip: Check your earnings dashboard regularly to monitor your available balance and payout status. See Track your earnings for details.
