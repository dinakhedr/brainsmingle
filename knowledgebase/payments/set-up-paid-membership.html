Set up paid membership

Last updated: July 2026

Charge a monthly or yearly subscription fee for your community. Enable paid membership when creating a community or update it later in your community settings. The platform handles billing and displays savings automatically for yearly plans.

Before you get started

  • You need an active BrainsMingle account.
  • You must connect your Stripe account to receive membership payouts. See Connect Stripe and receive payouts.

Enable paid membership

  1. When creating a community (or editing an existing one), check the Paid membership checkbox.
  2. Enter the Monthly Price in USD.

Offer yearly billing

  1. Check Offer yearly billing to give members a discounted annual option.
  2. Enter the Yearly Price in USD.
  3. The platform automatically calculates and displays the savings percentage compared to paying monthly.
Tip: You can change your membership pricing at any time from your community settings. Existing members keep their current billing cycle until renewal.
