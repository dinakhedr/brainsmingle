Set up paid membership

Last updated: August 2026

Charge members to join your community with monthly subscriptions, yearly billing, or one-time purchases. Set a grace period for expired subscriptions and attach discount codes to offer promotions. Enable paid membership when creating a community or update it later in your community settings.

Before you get started

  • You need an active BrainsMingle account.
  • You must connect your Stripe account to receive membership payouts. See Connect Stripe and receive payouts.
  • You need an existing community or be in the process of creating one. See Create a community.

Enable paid membership

When creating a community or editing an existing one from Settings > General, check the Paid membership checkbox. Three pricing options are available: monthly subscriptions, yearly billing, and one-time purchases. You can also set a grace period and attach discount codes.

Set a monthly price

  1. Check Monthly Price.
  2. Enter the price in USD.

Offer yearly billing (optional)

  1. Check Offer yearly billing.
  2. Enter the yearly price in USD. The platform automatically displays the savings percentage compared to paying monthly.

Set a grace period

The Grace period (days) field controls how long members keep access after their subscription ends. This gives members a buffer before losing access, which can help reduce churn from failed payments or delayed renewals. Set it to 0 to end access immediately when a subscription expires.

Offer a one-time purchase (optional)

Instead of a recurring subscription, you can let members pay once for access. Check Offer a one-time purchase, then enter the One-time price in USD.

Under How long does it give access?, choose one:

  • Forever — buyers keep access permanently.
  • For a set time — choose a preset duration (3 months, 6 months, or 9 months) or select Custom and enter a number of months.

Discount codes (optional)

The Discount codes section shows any codes that automatically apply to this community. If no codes exist yet, you can click Create a discount code to set one up on the Coupons page.

Tip: To create or manage discount codes, see Create and manage coupons. You can change your membership pricing at any time from your community settings. Existing members keep their current billing cycle until renewal.
