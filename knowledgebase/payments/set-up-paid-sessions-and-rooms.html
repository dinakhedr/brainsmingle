Set up paid sessions and rooms

Last updated: July 2026

Charge attendees for video sessions and speed networking rooms. Set a ticket price in USD, EGP, or both — the platform handles payment collection and shows you the net amount after the platform fee.

Before you get started

  • You must have an Admin or Moderator role to create sessions and rooms.
  • You must connect your Stripe account to receive payouts. See Connect Stripe and receive payouts.

Set up paid access for a video session

  1. When creating a video session, scroll to the Access & Pricing section.
  2. Under Paid Access, toggle from Free to Paid.
  3. Set a price in USD, EGP, or both. Attendees pay in the currency that fits them.
  4. The net amount you receive per attendee is displayed below the price field.
Please note: BrainsMingle charges a 10% platform fee on paid sessions. The net amount you receive per attendee is displayed below the price field.

Set up paid access for a speed networking room

  1. When creating a speed networking room, navigate to the Access & Privacy Settings step.
  2. Under Paid Event, toggle from Free to Paid.
  3. Set the ticket price.
Tip: You can also charge for consultation services separately. See Set up consultation services for pricing options.
