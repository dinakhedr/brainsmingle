Create a community

Last updated: August 2026

Launch a community space for any group or topic. Set the name, description, visibility, and membership rules. You can also enable paid membership with monthly or yearly billing.

Open the Communities page

  1. Click Communities in the main navigation.
  2. Click + Create Community in the top-right corner.

Set up your community

  1. Enter a Community Name. This is required.
  2. Optionally enter a Description to explain what your community is about.
  3. Under Community privacy, select one:
    • Private — invite only. Only people you invite can find and join.
    • Discoverable — anyone can find your community and request to join.
  4. Under How should new members join?, select one:
    • Auto-approve — anyone can join instantly.
    • Require approval — you review each request before members are added.
  5. Optionally select a Language for your community from the dropdown.
  6. Optionally add Tags to help members discover your community by topic.
  7. Optionally check Paid membership to charge a subscription fee. See Add paid membership below for full pricing options.

Add paid membership (optional)

Check the Paid membership checkbox to monetize your community. Three pricing options are available: monthly subscriptions, yearly billing, and one-time purchases.

Set a monthly price

  1. Check Monthly Price.
  2. Enter the price in USD.

Offer yearly billing (optional)

  1. Check Offer yearly billing.
  2. Enter the yearly price in USD. The platform automatically displays the savings percentage compared to paying monthly.

Offer a one-time purchase (optional)

Instead of a recurring subscription, you can let members pay once for access. Check Offer a one-time purchase, then enter the One-time price in USD.

Under How long does it give access?, choose one:

  • Forever — buyers keep access permanently.
  • For a set time — choose a preset duration (3 months, 6 months, or 9 months) or select Custom and enter a number of months.

Set a grace period

The Grace period (days) field controls how long members keep access after their subscription ends. This gives members a buffer before losing access, which can help reduce churn from failed payments or delayed renewals. Set it to 0 to end access immediately when a subscription expires.

Discount codes (optional)

The Discount codes section shows any codes that automatically apply to this community. If no codes exist yet, you can click Create a discount code to set one up. See Create and manage coupons for details.

Start your community

  1. Review your settings.
  2. Click Start Community.
Tip: You can change your community settings at any time after creation, including visibility, membership rules, and pricing.
