View your profile

Last updated: July 2026

Access your profile to view and edit your professional details, track your earnings, create discount coupons, share your public profile, invite your network, and manage your rooms, connections, feedback, and consultation services.

Open your profile

  1. Click your name in the top navigation bar.
  2. Select View Profile from the dropdown.

Your profile overview

Your profile page displays your photo, name, job title, follower and following counts, social links, website, and your About section. From here you can also access your earnings, coupons, public profile, profile link, and network invitations using the action buttons at the top.

  1. Your profile photo, name, username, and job title appear at the top. Click the pencil icon next to any field to edit it inline.
  2. Your followers and following counts are displayed below your name.
  3. Your LinkedIn and Website links appear below the counts. Click the pencil icon to edit them.
  4. The About section shows your bio. Click the pencil icon in the top-right corner of the section to edit it.

Track your earnings

  1. Click My Earnings in the top action bar of your profile.
  2. The dashboard shows four metrics:
    • Total Earnings — your lifetime earnings on the platform.
    • Available for Payout — the amount ready to be transferred to your bank account.
    • Pending Payout — payouts currently being processed.
    • Completed Payouts — total amount already deposited to your bank account.
  3. Click Connect Stripe to Receive Payouts to link your Stripe account and start receiving payments. See Connect Stripe and receive payouts.
  4. Click Create Paid Room to create a new room and start earning.
  5. Click Offer Consultation to set up your consultation services.
Tip: Check your earnings dashboard regularly to monitor your available balance and payout status.

Create and manage coupons

  1. Click Coupons in the top action bar of your profile.
  2. If you have no coupons yet, click Create Your First Coupon. Otherwise, click + Create Coupon in the top-right corner.
  1. In the Create New Coupon dialog, enter a Coupon Code (e.g., SAVE20).
  2. Enter a Discount Value as a percentage (e.g., 10 for 10% off).
  3. Optionally set Max Redemptions. Leave empty for unlimited uses.
  4. Under Applies To, select where the coupon can be used:
    • All Rooms, Consultations & Spaces — applies to everything.
    • All Rooms — applies to all your paid rooms.
    • All Consultations — applies to all your consultation services.
    • All Spaces — applies to all your spaces.
    • Specific Rooms — choose individual rooms.
    • Specific Consultations — choose individual consultation services.
    • Specific Spaces — choose individual spaces.
  1. If you selected a "Specific" option, check the items you want the coupon to apply to.
  1. Optionally set a Start Date and End Date to control when the coupon is valid.
  2. Click Create Coupon.

Manage your coupons

After creating coupons, they appear in a table with the following details: code, discount percentage, scope, uses, expiry date, and status.

  1. Use Search codes to find a specific coupon by its code.
  2. Filter by All statuses or All types using the dropdown filters.
  3. Click Activate or Deactivate to toggle a coupon on or off.
  4. Use the action icons to view usage, edit, or delete a coupon.

View your public profile

  1. Click View public profile in the top action bar of your profile.
  2. This opens your profile as other users see it, without edit controls.

Copy your profile link

  1. Click Copy profile link in the top action bar.
  2. Your profile URL is copied to your clipboard. Share it anywhere to direct people to your BrainsMingle profile.

Invite your network

  1. Click Invite Network in the top action bar.
  2. An invitation dialog appears with a pre-written message and your personal invite link.
  3. Click Copy invitation text to copy the message and link to your clipboard.
  4. Paste and share the invitation through any messaging app, email, or social platform.

Browse your profile tabs

The bottom section of your profile page contains five tabs that organize your activity on BrainsMingle.

Professional Network

View all the professionals you have connected with on BrainsMingle. Use the search bar to find connections by name, or sort by Recently met.

Rooms You Manage

View all the rooms you have created and manage on BrainsMingle. Each room card shows the event status, date, time, participant count, and description. Click Room Details to manage a room, or Share to share it.

Rooms You Joined

View all the rooms you have participated in as an attendee. Each card shows the event status, date, time, and a description. Click Room Details to view room information, or Share to share it.

Feedback

View professional feedback you have given and received from your connections. After meeting someone in a mingling room, you can leave feedback on their profile. Feedback you receive from others also appears here.

Services

Manage your consultation services. This tab shows all the 1-on-1 video consultation services you offer, including their visibility, duration, and pricing. You can also set your availability schedule and share your services on social media.

  1. Click Add New Service to create a new consultation service.
  2. Each service card shows its name, visibility (e.g., Private Link), duration, and price.
  3. Use the icons on each service card to duplicate, edit, or access more options.
  4. Under Availability Settings, your weekly availability is displayed. Click Edit Availability to update your schedule.
  5. Use the share icons next to Share your services to promote your services on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, or copy the link.
Tip: Retain 85–90% of your consultation fees on paid plans, and 85% on free plans. See the Services tab for current rates.
