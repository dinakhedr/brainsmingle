Understand roles and permissions

Last updated: July 2026

BrainsMingle uses four roles to control what each person can do inside a community. Each role has a different level of access to sessions, rooms, courses, and community settings.

Roles overview

Every community member is assigned one of the following roles:

  1. Admin — full control over the community. Admins can create and delete sessions, speed networking rooms, courses, and communities. They can manage members, configure community settings, connect payment accounts, and access analytics.
  2. Moderator — can create and manage sessions, speed networking rooms, and courses. Moderators can approve join requests and manage participants, but cannot delete the community or change community-level settings.
  3. Expert — can start instant drop-in calls and offer consultation services. Experts cannot create scheduled sessions or speed networking rooms.
  4. Member — can join sessions, speed networking rooms, and courses. Members can send direct messages to people they have met and participate in community discussions.
