Create a registration form

Last updated: July 2026

Require attendees to fill out a registration form before joining your video session or speed networking room. Use suggested fields for common information or add custom questions — the form is built right inside the room creation flow.

Before you get started

  • You must have an Admin or Moderator role.
  • Registration forms are available for video sessions, speed networking rooms and communities.

Enable the registration form

  1. During room creation, locate the Require attendees to fill a form toggle.
  2. Toggle it on.

Build your form

  1. Enter a Form title (e.g., "Registration for our session").
  2. Under Quick Add from Suggestions, click + next to any suggested fields to add them. Available suggestions include:
    • Full Name
    • Email Address
    • Phone Number
    • Company / Organization
    • Job Title
    • LinkedIn Profile URL
    • What brings you here?
    • How did you hear about us?
  3. To add a custom question, click + Add question and enter your question text.
Please note: You must add at least one question with text to enable publishing.
Tip: The same form builder is used for both video sessions and speed networking rooms. Once attendees submit the form, you can review their responses from the room detail page. See View and export form responses.
