View and export form responses

Last updated: July 2026

Review the registration form responses collected from attendees of your video sessions and speed networking rooms. Access all submissions from the room detail page.

Before you get started

  • You must have enabled a registration form when creating the room. See Create a registration form.
  • You must have an Admin or Moderator role.

View form responses

  1. Navigate to the room detail page by clicking the room title from your dashboard.
  2. Click View form responses below the room date and time.
  3. The collected registration form responses from attendees are displayed.
Please note: The View form responses link only appears if you enabled the registration form when creating the room.
Tip: Review form responses before the event to understand your audience and prepare relevant discussion topics.
