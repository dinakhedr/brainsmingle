Event Sponsorship

A native sponsorship engine no competitor has.

Create sponsor records with full branding — logo, card image, description, link, and a custom call-to-action button. Assign sponsors to any event and control exactly where their branding appears. This is a revenue layer built directly into your event infrastructure, not a third-party integration or a manual workaround.

  • Full sponsor profile — title, description, logo, card image, link, CTA button, and display order.
  • Custom CTAs — each sponsor gets its own button label (e.g. "Learn More", "Visit IBM").
  • Visibility controls — toggle "Card can be shown" and set display order per sponsor.
A completed sponsor record — branding, link, and CTA configured
Sponsor Tiers

Sell tiered sponsorship packages at different price points.

Assign each sponsor a tier — Gold, Silver, or Bronze. Tiers let you build structured sponsorship packages: Gold gets all 9 placements, Silver gets 5, Bronze gets the logo bar. You define the packages, set the pricing, and the platform delivers the placements.

  • Gold, Silver, Bronze — built-in tier system for structured sponsorship pricing.
  • Multiple sponsors per event — assign as many sponsors as the event needs.
  • Status management — each sponsor shows Active or Inactive status with full control.
Multiple sponsors with tier assignments — Gold and Silver on the same event
Placement Types

Nine placement types across two surfaces.

Control exactly where each sponsor appears. Toggle placements independently for the Event Details Page and for Associated Rooms — so a Gold sponsor can have the Banner Hero on the event page and the Header Logo inside the live session, while a Bronze sponsor only gets the Logo Bar.

  • Logo Bar — horizontal logo strip across the event page.
  • Banner Hero & Skyscraper — high-impact placements for premium sponsors.
  • Floating Notification & Message Strip — attention-grabbing formats that overlay content.
  • Native Card, Inline Compact, Sidebar Tile, Header Logo — seamless placements that match your platform's design.
The Placements tab — 9 types toggled independently per sponsor, per surface
Reusable Sponsors

Save a sponsor once, assign them to every event.

Once a sponsor record is created for one event, it becomes available in the Reusable Sponsor Data library. Running a weekly session series with the same Gold sponsor? Assign them across all events in seconds — no re-entering logos, links, or CTA details.

  • Sponsor library — all saved sponsors available for reuse across events.
  • One-click assignment — click Choose to assign a saved sponsor to a new event.
  • Independent placements — reuse the sponsor record but configure placements differently per event.
A saved sponsor ready for reuse — Choose to assign to another event
Paid Ticketing

Charge for any event with one toggle.

Toggle Paid Access during event creation and set a price in USD, EGP, or both. Attendees pay before they join. The platform handles payment collection, shows the net amount after the platform fee, and the same flow works for video sessions and speed networking rooms.

  • Dual currency — set prices in USD, EGP, or both. Attendees choose what fits them.
  • Transparent fees — net earnings per attendee displayed below the price field.
  • Coupon support — apply discount codes scoped to specific events or all events.
Paid Access enabled — price field with net earnings breakdown
Registration & Gating

Know your attendees before the event starts.

Attach a registration form to any event during creation. Pick from suggested fields — name, email, company, job title, LinkedIn — or add custom questions. Attendees must complete the form before they can join, so you always know who is in the room.

  • Suggested fields — one-click to add Full Name, Email, Phone, Company, Job Title, and LinkedIn URL.
  • Custom questions — add open-ended questions specific to the event.
  • Response review — view all submissions from the room detail page before or after the event.
The built-in form builder — suggested fields and custom questions
Participant Management

Invite, approve, and manage every attendee.

Share the event link with one click, invite participants by email with a custom message, manage the waitlist for approval-required events, and track who has joined. The room detail page is your event command center — participants, waitlist, recordings, and discussions in one place.

  • Email invitations — invite up to 5 people at once with an optional custom message.
  • Waitlist management — review and approve pending attendees for gated events.
  • Shareable link — copy the event link and distribute it via any channel.
Email invitations — add addresses and an optional custom message
Attendance Cards & Sharing

Shareable event cards your audience can pass along.

Download a branded attendance card from any room detail page — a visual card with event details ready to share on social media, email, or messaging. Combined with the shareable link and email invitations, every event has the distribution tools it needs built in.

  • Downloadable cards — click Get Attendance Card to download a shareable event visual.
  • Copy link — share the event URL via any channel with one click.
  • Form responses & recordings — access attendee data, session recordings, and post-event discussions from one page.
The room detail page — link bar, attendance card, form responses, and recordings
