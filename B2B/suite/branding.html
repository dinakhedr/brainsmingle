Custom Domain

Your domain in every link, every browser tab.

Deploy your Suite on your own domain — learn.yourbrand.com, community.youracademy.org, or whatever fits your organization. Every session link, every invite, every shared URL carries your identity. Your audience never sees a third-party URL.

  • Your URL — every page, session, and course lives on your custom domain.
  • SSL included — secure HTTPS connection configured automatically.
  • Shareable links — every invitation and event link uses your domain, reinforcing your brand with every share.
Logo & Brand Colors

Upload your logo and set your colors.

Your logo appears in the navigation, on session screens, in email notifications, and across every member-facing surface. Set your primary and accent colors so buttons, highlights, and UI elements match your brand system — not ours.

  • Logo upload — your logo in the nav bar, favicon, and all branded surfaces.
  • Brand colors — primary and accent colors applied to buttons, links, and UI elements.
  • Favicon — your icon in browser tabs and bookmarks.
  • Consistent identity — every screen, every email, every link matches your brand.
Languages & Arabic/RTL

Deploy in the language your audience speaks.

Enable any combination of 6 languages — English, Arabic, Urdu, Turkish, Spanish, and French — and set a default for new visitors. Arabic includes full RTL layout support built into the platform, not added as a plugin. Members switch languages from a picker at any time.

  • 6 languages — English, Arabic, Urdu, Turkish, Spanish, and French.
  • Full RTL support — Arabic and Urdu render with native right-to-left layout across the entire platform.
  • Default language — set which language new visitors see when their browser language is not in your supported list.
  • Member language picker — members switch languages anytime from the platform interface.
The Languages page — 6 languages with support toggles and default selection
Social Links

Connect your social presence to your platform.

Add your organization's social media profiles and they appear as icons in the platform footer. Toggle on the networks you use, paste each URL, and your audience sees a professional "Get in Touch" section — all pointing to your channels, not ours.

  • 7 networks — Facebook, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp.
  • Toggle controls — enable only the networks you use. Disabled networks are hidden from the footer.
  • Preserved URLs — disable a network without losing your URL. Re-enable it later without re-entering.
Social Links — toggle networks on, paste your profile URLs
Professional Profiles

Every member has a visible professional identity.

Members set up their own profiles with a photo, name, job title, company, bio, website, and social links. Their profile becomes their identity across the platform — visible in sessions, communities, networking rounds, and the member directory.

  • Professional details — job title, company, bio, website, LinkedIn, and X handle.
  • Job status — Employed, Not Looking, or Open to Work — with a toggle to show or hide on their profile.
  • Cross-platform visibility — the same profile appears in sessions, communities, networking, and the directory.
