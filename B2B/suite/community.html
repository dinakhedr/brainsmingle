Community Creation

Launch a community in under a minute.

Name your community, write a description, set visibility and membership rules, and you're live. Every community comes with default channels, a member directory, and a customizable sidebar — ready from day one.

  • Private or Discoverable — invite-only communities or open-to-find with request-to-join.
  • Auto-approve or require approval — let anyone join instantly or review each request manually.
  • Unlimited communities — create as many as your program needs, organized by topic, cohort, or sector.
The Create Space dialog — name, description, visibility, and membership rules
Branding & Identity

Your brand, not ours.

Upload a community icon and cover image, edit the name and description, and write a rich-text About page that non-members see first. Every community looks like it was built by your organization — because it was.

  • Custom icon & cover — square icon (up to 2 MB) and a wide cover image (1200×400 recommended).
  • Rich-text About page — formatted text, images, videos, and links to introduce your community to visitors.
  • First-impression control — non-members see your About page before anything else.
General Settings — branding, name, description, and visibility
Topic Channels

Structure the conversation, don't scatter it.

Every community starts with three default channels — # general, # announcements, and # resources. Add custom channels for any topic, and lock channels to announcement-only so only admins can post official updates.

  • Unlimited custom channels — add channels for projects, regions, cohorts, or any structure you need.
  • Announcement-only mode — restrict posting to admins for official communications.
  • Rename or remove — default channels can be edited or deleted to match your vocabulary.
The Channels tab — default channels with the option to add more
Access Control

Decide who gets in and how.

Three membership modes give you the right level of control: auto-approve anyone instantly, criteria-based rules that accept or reject automatically based on form answers, or manual approval where you review every request.

  • Three join modes — auto-approve, criteria-based, or require approval.
  • Criteria-based gating — auto-approve applicants whose answers meet your defined rules.
  • Visibility + access — combine Private/Discoverable visibility with any join mode.
Membership rules — auto-approve, criteria-based, or manual approval
Join Forms

Collect the data you need before they join.

Require new members to fill a custom form before gaining access. Use suggested fields — full name, email, company, job title, LinkedIn — or add your own questions. Every response is reviewable from the Members tab.

  • Quick-add suggestions — Full Name, Email, Phone, Company, Job Title, LinkedIn, and more.
  • Custom questions — add your own with answer type and required/optional toggle.
  • Reviewable responses — access all form submissions directly from the Members tab.
Join form builder — suggested fields and custom questions
Roles & Governance

Manage your members without managing chaos.

View all members as cards with name, role badge, and join date. Promote members to Admin or Moderator, approve or reject pending requests, add members directly, and ban anyone who violates your guidelines.

  • Three roles — Admin (full control), Moderator (manage sessions, rooms, courses), Member.
  • Pending queue — approve or reject join requests with one click.
  • Direct invite — add members by name or email without waiting for them to request access.
  • Ban members — remove and prevent re-entry with a single action.
Member management — role assignment from the member card menu
Paid Membership

Earn from your community, natively.

Charge a monthly subscription fee with one checkbox. Offer yearly billing at a discounted rate and the platform automatically calculates and displays the savings percentage. No Stripe integrations. No payment plugins. Revenue collection is built in.

  • Monthly pricing — set your price in USD and start collecting immediately.
  • Yearly billing — offer a discounted annual option with auto-calculated savings display.
  • Toggle on or off — convert any free community to paid, or remove pricing, at any time.
Paid membership — monthly and yearly pricing with auto-calculated savings
Navigation

Show only what your community needs.

Drag tabs to reorder the sidebar, hide tabs your community doesn't use, and set the default landing page. If your community doesn't offer courses yet, hide the Courses tab — members won't see it. Show it again whenever you're ready.

  • Drag-and-drop reorder — move Feed, Rooms, Courses, and Members into any order.
  • Show or hide — toggle visibility per tab. Hidden tabs are fully removed from the sidebar.
  • Default landing page — the first visible tab becomes the page members see when they open your community.
Navigation settings — tab order, visibility, and default landing page
Embedded Events

Run events inside the community, not outside it.

Create rooms directly from your community homepage — no separate event pages, no external links. Choose a Mingling Room for structured speed networking with lobby and matching, or a Video Call for a simple group meeting. Events stay scoped to the community that created them.

  • Mingling Room — lobby, matching algorithm, and timed 1:1 video rounds inside the community.
  • Video Call — a direct group video meeting without matching or lobby.
  • Community-scoped — rooms appear in the community's Rooms tab, visible only to members.
Create a room — Mingling Room for speed networking or Video Call for group meetings
