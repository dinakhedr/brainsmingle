Room Creation

Launch a networking room in under a minute.

Click + Create Room, select Speed-Networking Room, enter a title and description, and you're ready to configure the experience. The platform handles matchmaking, timing, and transitions automatically.

  • Three room types — choose from Instant Video Call, Video Room, or Speed-Networking Room.
  • Title & description — tell participants what to expect and suggest discussion topics.
  • Step-by-step flow — purpose, experience design, scheduling, and access settings in one guided wizard.
The Create Room dropdown — select Speed-Networking Room to begin
Role-Based Matching

Pair the right people with the right people.

Enable role-based matching to define two roles — Investor and Founder, Mentor and Mentee, Employer and Candidate — and choose whether to match opposites or same roles. Without roles, the platform defaults to random matching with no repeats.

  • Opposite mode — Role 1 always meets Role 2. Ideal for founder–investor or student–alumni.
  • Same mode — Role 1 meets Role 1. Ideal for peer-to-peer exchange within a cohort.
  • Random fallback — without roles enabled, the platform matches randomly with no repeats.
Role Configuration — define two roles and choose Opposite or Same matching
Experience Design

Control the flow from start to finish.

Set the total room duration (20–60 minutes) and 1:1 call length (3–15 minutes per round). Optionally add a 5-minute intro room for a group welcome and an outro call for closing remarks. The platform calculates the maximum number of conversations automatically.

  • Intro room — a 5-minute group call at the start before matching begins.
  • Outro call — a 5-minute group call at the end for wrap-up and announcements.
  • Auto-calculated rounds — a 30-minute room with 5-minute calls gives up to 6 conversations.
Experience Design — intro room, total duration, and 1:1 call length controls
Scheduling

Set it once. It repeats on its own.

Pick a date and timezone — participants see the event in their local time automatically. Toggle Recurring Room on and select a repeat frequency. Set an optional end date or let it run indefinitely. Each occurrence uses the same settings — title, duration, call length, and access rules.

  • Five frequencies — Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, or Custom Days.
  • Custom day picker — select specific days of the week for your repeat schedule.
  • Configure once — every future occurrence inherits the same settings automatically.
All recurrence options — from daily to custom day selection
Access & Privacy

Control who joins and how they register.

Every room has independent controls for privacy, discoverability, and pricing. Auto-approve or require manual approval, list in search or keep unlisted, and make the event free or paid. Attach a registration form to collect attendee data before the event.

  • Room Privacy — auto-approve anyone with the link, or require manual approval.
  • Free or paid — set a ticket price or keep the room free. Tags and spoken language optional.
  • Registration forms — collect full name, email, company, job title, LinkedIn URL, or add custom questions.
Access & Privacy — visibility, pricing, language, and registration form
Room Management

Share, invite, and manage before the event.

The room detail page is your command center. Copy the shareable link, invite participants by email with a custom message, download the attendance card, view registration form responses, and manage the participant list and waitlist — all in one place.

  • Copy & Invite — share the link via clipboard or invite up to 5 people by email with a personal note.

  • Members & waitlist — view confirmed participants, manage the waitlist, and access recordings and discussions.

Live Matching

One click to meet someone new.

Click Meet someone new and the platform finds your match instantly. A "Connecting you…" animation plays while the algorithm pairs you into a 1:1 video call. Each person is matched only once — no repeats. When the timer runs out, the call ends and you're ready for the next round.

  • No-repeat matching — the algorithm ensures you never meet the same person twice.
  • Participant status — see who's Available, In Call, or waiting in the sidebar.
  • Cancel any time — stop the matching process before a connection is made.
The matching animation — finding your next 1:1 connection
Email Invitations

Invite participants directly from the platform.

Open the Invite dialog from the room detail page, add up to 5 email addresses per invitation, include an optional custom message (up to 500 characters), and send. Recipients receive a branded invitation with the room link and your personal note.

  • Batch invitations — add up to 5 email addresses per send.
  • Custom message — add a personal note to give context about the networking event.
  • Branded delivery — invitations carry your platform's branding automatically.
Invite Guests by Email — add addresses, write a note, and send
Registration Forms

Know your participants before the session starts.

Attach a registration form to any speed networking room. Choose from suggested fields — full name, email, company, job title, LinkedIn URL — or add custom questions. Responses are collected and exportable before the event begins.

  • Quick-add suggestions — one-click to add common fields like Name, Email, Company, Job Title.
  • Custom questions — add open-ended questions specific to your networking event.
  • Export responses — download form data to review attendee profiles before the event.
The built-in form builder with quick-add suggestions and custom questions
Connection Summary

Every connection persists after the event.

When a speed networking session ends, every participant receives a connection summary — a list of everyone they met, with their name, headline, and profile link. Click a name to view their full profile and send a direct message. Connections outlast the event.

  • Automatic generation — the summary is created for every participant as soon as the session ends.
  • Direct messaging — follow up instantly from the summary with built-in messaging.
  • Admin view — hosts see the full participant list via the Members tab and post-event discussions.
Room detail page — access Members and Discussions tabs for post-event follow-up
Event Sponsorship

Sponsor your networking events natively.

Add sponsors to any speed networking room from the Admin Panel. Configure sponsor branding — logo, card image, description, and call-to-action button — then control placements on the event details page and inside the room. Reuse sponsor records across events.

  • 9 placement types — Logo Bar, Skyscraper, Inline Compact, Header Logo, Floating Notification, Native Card, Banner Hero, Sidebar Tile, and Message Strip.
  • Tiered sponsors — assign Gold, Silver, or Bronze tiers to each sponsor.
  • Reusable records — save a sponsor once, reuse across any future event.
Sponsorship Placements — toggle placement types per sponsor, per location
