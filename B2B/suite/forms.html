Form Builder

Build a form in seconds — no separate tool needed.

The form builder is embedded directly in the room and community creation flow. Enter a title, pick from suggested fields, or write custom questions. The same builder works identically for video sessions, speed networking rooms, and community join forms.

  • Suggested fields — one-click to add Full Name, Email, Phone, Company, Job Title, LinkedIn URL, and more.
  • Custom questions — add open-ended questions specific to your event or community.
  • Required fields — mark any question as mandatory so attendees cannot skip it.
  • One builder, everywhere — the same form experience across sessions, networking rooms, and communities.

Session Registration

Require a form before anyone joins.

Toggle on "Require attendees to fill a form" during session or room creation. Attendees must complete the form before they can access the event. You see their responses before the session even starts — so you know exactly who is in the room.

  • One toggle — enable forms during creation, no configuration page needed.
  • Video sessions — gate access to scheduled sessions, webinars, and instant calls.
  • Speed networking rooms — collect attendee info before matchmaking begins.
The registration form toggle — one switch to enable attendee data collection
Community Join Forms

Screen members before they enter your community.

Attach a join form to any community. New members fill it out when they request to join. Choose how applications are handled — auto-approve everyone, manually review each one, or set criteria-based rules that auto-approve or auto-reject based on answers.

  • Auto-approve — anyone can join instantly, no review needed.
  • Criteria-based — define rules to auto-approve or auto-reject applicants based on their form answers.
  • Manual approval — review each application individually before granting access.
  • Answer types — choose short answer, structured responses, or required fields per question.
The community join form — suggested fields, custom questions, and answer type controls
Join Methods

Three ways to control who gets in.

Every community gives you a choice: open the doors, set the rules, or review every request. Combine join methods with form data to build exactly the admission flow your program needs — from open enrollment to curated cohorts.

  • Open communities — auto-approve for public programs where access should be frictionless.
  • Curated access — require approval for invite-only cohorts or premium memberships.
  • Automated screening — criteria-based rules handle admission at scale without manual review.
Join method selection — auto-approve, criteria-based, or require approval
Response Collection

Every submission in a table, exportable in one click.

All form submissions appear in a structured table with each question as a column — name, email, phone, custom answers, and submission timestamp. Download the full dataset as CSV or Excel for reporting, CRM import, or offline review.

  • Structured table — each form field becomes a column. Every submission is a row with a timestamp.
  • CSV & Excel export — download the full dataset in one click for reporting or CRM import.
  • Community & event forms — the same submissions view works for join forms, session registration, and networking rooms.
