Structured Programs

Organize courses into structured programs.

Create a program, add courses to it, and fill each course with lessons. Learners enroll once and access every course in one place. Programs show cover images, descriptions, and course counts — ready for your audience to browse and start learning.

  • Programs & standalone courses — group related courses under a program or create standalone courses that exist independently.
  • Draft & Published — build in draft mode until everything is ready, then publish to your members.
  • Cover images & descriptions — every program and course has its own branded card with visual identity.
The New program dialog — title, description, cover image, and visibility
Course Management

Add as many courses as your program needs.

Each program holds unlimited courses. Assign a course to a program or let it stand alone. Set visibility, add cover images, and optionally enable completion certificates — all from the course creation dialog.

  • Program assignment — assign courses to any existing program or keep them standalone.
  • Completion certificate toggle — enable per-course certificates that are awarded when all lessons are completed.
  • Unlimited courses — no cap on how many courses you can create across your deployment.
The New course dialog — program assignment, visibility, and certificate toggle
Video Lessons

Upload videos or embed from YouTube and Vimeo.

Add video lessons by uploading MP4 files directly to secure storage or by pasting an embed link from YouTube or Vimeo. Set a duration for each lesson — it feeds into total learning hours for certification. Every uploaded video is stored securely within your deployment.

  • Direct upload — upload MP4 files to secure, platform-managed storage.
  • Embed URL — paste an unlisted YouTube or Vimeo link for externally hosted content.
  • Duration tracking — set minutes per lesson to calculate total learning hours across the program.
Video lesson with Upload MP4 — file upload, duration, and visibility settings
Text Lessons

Write lessons with a full rich text editor.

Not every lesson needs a video. Create text-based lessons using the built-in rich text editor — format with bold, italic, and headings, add links, embed images and videos, and structure content exactly the way your subject matter requires.

  • Rich formatting — bold, italic, headings, lists, links, images, and embedded videos.
  • Mixed lesson types — combine video and text lessons in the same course for a blended experience.
  • Draft & publish per lesson — control visibility at the lesson level, not just the course level.
Text lesson — rich text editor with bold, headings, links, images, and video embed
Certification

Award certificates automatically when learners finish.

Upload a custom certificate background, choose dark or light text, and set completion thresholds — minimum courses completed, minimum learning hours, or both. The platform issues certificates automatically when a learner meets either threshold. Each certificate includes the recipient name, course title, completion date, a unique certificate ID, and a verification link.

  • Custom certificate design — upload your branded background in landscape A4 (1600×1131 px).
  • Flexible thresholds — set minimum courses, minimum hours, or both. Meet either and the certificate is issued.
  • Verifiable — every certificate includes a unique ID and verification link for third-party validation.
Custom certificate design — upload, text color, and live preview
Learner Analytics

See exactly where every learner stands.

Two levels of analytics — all-courses and per-course. The all-courses dashboard shows total enrollment, course count, average completion, and certification rates across your entire program. Drill into any course to see individual learner progress, lesson completion counts, and last activity timestamps.

  • Summary cards — Members, Courses, Avg completion, and Certified at a glance.
  • Filters — narrow by courses completed, overall percentage, certificate status, or date range.
  • Export — download learner data as CSV or Excel for reporting or offline review.
The all-courses analytics dashboard — summary cards, filters, and member table
Playback Controls

Enforce structured viewing when it matters.

Enable restricted playback to prevent learners from skipping ahead in course videos. When active, fast-forwarding, speed changes, and seek bar scrubbing are disabled until the lesson is fully watched. The Mark Complete button stays locked until the video finishes. Once completed, full seeking is unlocked on rewatch.

  • No skip-ahead — learners watch in full before progressing. Back 10 sec remains available.
  • Completion-gated — Mark Complete is disabled until the video finishes playing.
  • Rewatch freedom — once a lesson is completed, seeking and speed controls are fully unlocked.
